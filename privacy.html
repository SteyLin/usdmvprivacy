Privacy Policy

DMV Practice Test 2026

Effective date: August 1, 2026

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information for users of the DMV Practice Test 2026 mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email, or phone number to use its core features (practice questions, exams, progress tracking).

However, the App uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC ("Google"). AdMob may collect and process the following data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Approximate location (coarse, city-level)
  • App usage and interaction data
  • General device information (model, OS version)

For more information on how Google handles data, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's features (questions, exams, saved progress).
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • To improve the App and user experience.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising

Ads in the App are served by Google AdMob. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization) or via https://adssettings.google.com/.

4. Data Retention

Practice progress, favorites, and wrong-answer history are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore. This data is not transmitted to us and can be cleared by uninstalling the App.

Advertising identifiers are handled by Google according to its own retention policies.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users preparing for a driver's license written knowledge exam. It is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because we store practice data only on your device, you may clear it at any time by uninstalling the App. For advertising data handled by Google, please use Google's privacy tools linked above.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App and on this page.
